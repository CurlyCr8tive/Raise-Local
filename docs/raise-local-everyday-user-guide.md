# Raise Local Everyday User Guide

This guide is for Tenyse's normal day-to-day use of Raise Local after the
handoff. It is separate from the presentation and demo instructions.

## Sign In

1. Open the production Raise Local link provided during handoff.
2. Select **Log in**.
3. Enter your email address and password.
4. If you cannot remember your password, choose **Forgot password** and follow
   the email instructions.
5. After signing in, confirm that the account menu shows your admin identity.

Do not use the demo workspace for live client records. The demo reset is for
rehearsals only and does not delete live Supabase data.

## Dashboard

The dashboard is the starting point for daily review. Use it to see:

- New campaign requests.
- Business profiles that need review.
- Potential and approved matches.
- Partnerships that are active.
- Notifications and recent activity.

Start with the notification indicator and any records marked as needing
review. Open the related record rather than relying only on the summary count.

## Campaign Requests

Campaign Requests contains nonprofit, school, and community requests submitted
through Raise Local.

### Review a request

1. Open **Campaign Requests**.
2. Select **View details** on the request.
3. Review the organization, mission, campaign description, cause, location,
   dates, goal, participation, support needs, and partnership type.
4. Check whether the contact details and campaign information are complete.
5. Select **Edit profile** if information needs to be corrected or completed.
6. Save the changes and confirm the success message.

### Add a request for a client

Select **Add campaign request** and complete the form on the client's behalf.
Use the client's organization name and contact email so the record is
associated with the correct client account.

## Business Profiles

Business Profiles contains businesses available for matching with campaigns.

### Review a business

1. Open **Business Profiles**.
2. Select **View details**.
3. Review the business category, location, service areas, causes, offer type,
   contribution type, capacity, availability, pricing, and fulfillment options.
4. Confirm that the business can realistically support the campaigns being
   considered.
5. Select **Edit profile** to correct or complete the record.

### Add a business for a client

Select **Add business profile** and enter the client's confirmed information.
Do not create a public-business lead as a confirmed partner. A business must
submit or confirm its own profile before it is treated as a participating
partner.

## Match Review

Match Review is where potential partnerships are evaluated.

1. Open **Match Review**.
2. Review the nonprofit and business names.
3. Open **View details**.
4. Read the compatibility score and the explanation of why the match fits.
5. Review the decision path: availability, location, cause, business type,
   partnership type, offer fit, capacity, timing, and financial minimums.
6. Review the suggested campaign approach and fundraising scenario.
7. Add an admin note when additional context or judgment is needed.

A suggested match is not a confirmed partnership. It becomes ready for
outreach only after both sides approve.

## Match Decisions

The available decisions are:

- **Approve:** the organization is open to moving forward with the match.
- **Hold:** pause the match while waiting for information or timing.
- **Decline:** close the match and record the reason.

When declining, select the closest reason and add a short note when useful.
Use notes to preserve context for future review.

After the nonprofit and business both approve:

1. Confirm that the match shows mutual approval.
2. Review the notification and next-step context.
3. Open **Outreach** to prepare the introduction.

## Outreach

Outreach is the relationship coordination workspace after mutual approval.

1. Open **Outreach**.
2. Select the approved partnership.
3. Review the suggested introduction and campaign context.
4. Edit the message so it reflects the actual organizations, goals, dates, and
   requested next step.
5. Send the outreach when it is ready.
6. Record the contact or follow-up status.

Do not send an introduction based only on a suggested match. Confirm mutual
approval first.

## My Projects

My Projects is the ongoing record of partnerships that have moved beyond
matching.

Use it to:

- Confirm that the partnership has been accepted.
- Record the agreed campaign or event details.
- Track outreach and follow-up.
- Mark the partnership as confirmed.
- Move the campaign to **Active** once both sides have agreed on the plan.
- Keep the campaign context, goals, dates, and next actions together.

An active project should contain enough information for someone else on the
team to understand how the nonprofit and business will work together and how
the campaign goal will be reached.

## Editing Records

Campaign requests and business profiles are shared records.

- Clients can edit records associated with their email address.
- Tenyse and authorized admins can edit client-created records.
- The latest approved value becomes the live value.
- Admin edits retain edit history, including who changed the record and when.
- Separate campaigns remain separate records even when they belong to the same
  organization.

After editing, confirm the success message and refresh the page to verify that
the change persisted.

## Notifications

Notifications may appear in the application and by email when:

- A new match is created.
- One side approves a match.
- Both sides approve a match.
- Outreach is sent.
- A campaign request or business profile changes.

If an expected email does not arrive, check the in-app notification first and
contact the technical owner with the record name, action, and approximate time.
Do not resend repeatedly while troubleshooting.

## Daily Review Routine

1. Check notifications.
2. Review new campaign requests.
3. Review new or changed business profiles.
4. Open Match Review and handle suggested matches.
5. Follow up on matches that are on hold.
6. Review mutual approvals in Outreach.
7. Update confirmed or active projects.
8. Check Reports for activity that needs to be shared or followed up.

## What To Keep Accurate

Before a match is approved, confirm:

- Contact email and name.
- Organization or business name.
- Location and service area.
- Cause and campaign purpose.
- Campaign dates and deadlines.
- Capacity and participation expectations.
- Contribution or partnership type.
- Pricing or financial minimums.
- The next action each side is expected to take.

Raise Local can explain compatibility, but it does not guarantee fundraising
results, business quality, nonprofit eligibility, or partnership success.
